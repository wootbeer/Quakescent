# Quakescent 0.1.0

First release.

- Fly Descent's Pyro-GX through Quake on an unmodified FTEQW: full 6DOF
  flight with Descent's physics, its cockpit and gauges, the reticle, the
  rear view and the message line
- Descent 1's weapons, with their models and effects: lasers (levels 1-4
  and quad), vulcan, spreadfire, plasma and fusion; concussion, homing,
  smart and mega missiles, proximity bombs and flares
- Quake's pickups become Descent's spinning powerups
- Descent II mode (`Quakescent2.bat`): Descent II's high-resolution
  cockpit, its sounds, weapons and equipment (afterburner, headlight,
  energy converter, ammo rack)
- Descent's cheat codes, typed during play. Quake's own cheats still work
- Getting hit knocks the ship around, and shots light up the walls as they
  fly, as in Descent
- Options menu (F7): cockpit, turning style, mouse momentum, thrust,
  weapon damage and damage taken
- The converter reads registered (1.4/1.5) and shareware (1.0-1.2)
  Descent 1 data, and Descent II's
- Deathmatch works (lightly tested)
