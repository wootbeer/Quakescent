# 0.1.0

First version.

- The Pyro-GX in Quake on FTEQW: 6DOF flight with Descent's physics, the
  cockpit and gauges, the reticle, the rear view and the message line.
- Descent 1's weapons (lasers 1-4 and quad, vulcan, spreadfire, plasma,
  fusion; concussion, homing, smart, proximity, mega; flares) with its
  weapon models and effects.
- Quake's pickups as Descent's powerups, spinning at Descent's speed.
- Quakescent options menu (F7), with weapon damage, damage taken and Ick
  Mode.
- Descent 1's cheat codes, typed during play (and Doomscent's astral,
  buggin and bittersweet).
- Ick Mode: monsters wear a face, bleed green and warp out.
- Lasers and other weapons cast coloured light as they fly, as in Descent.
- The converter reads registered (1.4/1.5) and shareware (1.0-1.2)
  Descent 1 data.
